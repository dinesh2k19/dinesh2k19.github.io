01 · About

Science with an impact on global health.

I work at the intersection of microbial genomics, antimicrobial resistance, epidemiology and computational biology.

I am a first-generation doctoral graduate who earned my Ph.D. in Biotechnology (2017–2023) from Gujarat University, Ahmedabad, supported by the Gujarat Biotechnology Research Centre (GBRC) under the Department of Science and Technology, Government of Gujarat.

My research interests include computational biology, bioinformatics, NGS data analysis, antimicrobial resistance, microbial genomics, pathogen surveillance, SARS-CoV-2, epidemiology and host–microbiome interactions, with an aim to make an impact in global health.

Previously, I served as a Post-doctoral Research Fellow at BITS Pilani, Hyderabad, working on lipids and cyclic-nucleotide-mediated regulation of the lytic cycle in Toxoplasma gondii.

My earlier work includes environmental microbiomes, food-borne pathogens, antimicrobial resistance and SARS-CoV-2 genomic surveillance at Gujarat Biotechnology Research Centre.
