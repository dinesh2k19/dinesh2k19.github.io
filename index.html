About Me

I am a biotechnology researcher working at the intersection of microbial genomics, antimicrobial resistance, epidemiology and computational biology.

I am a first-generation doctoral graduate who earned my Ph.D. in Biotechnology from Gujarat University, Ahmedabad, supported by the Gujarat Biotechnology Research Centre (GBRC) under the Department of Science and Technology, Government of Gujarat.

My current research focuses on the de-novo origin of the antimicrobial resistome and its evolutionary trajectory in microbial pathogens. My broader interests include computational biology, bioinformatics, NGS data analysis, microbial genomics, pathogen surveillance, SARS-CoV-2, epidemiology and host–microbiome interactions.

Current Research

Antimicrobial Resistance

Evolution, surveillance and genomic investigation of antimicrobial resistance determinants and the resistome.

Microbial Genomics

Genome analysis, comparative genomics, pathogen surveillance and evolutionary studies using NGS datasets.

Computational Biology

Bioinformatics workflows, metagenomics, phylogenetics and data-driven biological discovery.

Human, animal and environmental microbiomes, zoonoses and public-health surveillance.
